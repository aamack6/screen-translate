# ScreenTranslate privacy policy

ScreenTranslate does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

- Screenshots, photos and camera shots you translate are processed on your device by Apple's Vision and
  Translation frameworks. They are never uploaded. The camera is only used when you tap "Translate with the camera".
- If you choose an online translation engine (DeepL or Google) and enter your own API key, the text read from the
  screen, but never the image, is sent to that service to be translated, under its own privacy policy. The app asks
  before it sends anything to a service for the first time, and never switches to an online service on its own:
  if the online engine fails, it uses the on-device one. Only the text being translated is sent (and, for DeepL, the
  other text on the same screen as context). The key is kept in your device's Keychain. With the default engine,
  nothing leaves your device.
- The live camera reads text on your device and keeps no photos or video. The built-in browser loads the websites
  you open like any browser; the text it translates is handled the same way as screenshot text above.
  Websites can store cookies and data in the app, as they do in Safari.
- Your word list is stored only on your device.
- Screenshots are not saved to your photo library unless you choose to save or share a result.
- If you turn on History, recent translations are kept in the app's private storage on your device. Turning it off
  or tapping Clear deletes them. Deleting the app deletes them too. History is not included in iCloud or computer
  backups.
- To make repeat translations instant, the app remembers recently translated text (not images) in its private
  cache on your device. You can turn this off with "Remember recent translations". Clear in History empties it, and
  iOS may also clear it when storage is low. The cache is not backed up.
- If you turn on price conversion, the app downloads exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) about
  twice a day. The request contains only a currency code such as "JPY", never your screenshots or text.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting of its own, and doesn't write what you
  translate to logs.
